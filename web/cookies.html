Cookie policy

This site does not use advertising cookies, social pixels, or analytics that phone home by default.

Essential session cookie

When you sign in to the hosted API, the server may set pwmanager_session: an HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict cookie holding an opaque token. It authenticates later requests. It is not used for marketing. It expires on a rolling window (about 14 days) or when you sign out. This cookie is strictly necessary for the cloud account to work.

Local storage (not a cookie)

The generator stores interface preferences (theme, lengths) under pwmanager.web.prefs.v1. The SaaS UI stores theme under pwmanager.saas.theme. IndexedDB holds ciphertext envelopes for your vault, never the master password. These stay on your device.

Consent

We do not set non-essential cookies. If we ever add optional analytics, they will stay off until you opt in. Stripe Checkout on stripe.com uses Stripe’s own cookies; that is Stripe’s processing, described in their disclosures, after you leave this origin.

How to clear

Sign out to drop the session cookie. Clear site data in your browser to remove local ciphertext — keep an encrypted backup first if you still need the vault.
