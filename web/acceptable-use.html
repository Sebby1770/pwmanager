Acceptable use

You may use pwmanager to store your own credentials and notes. You may not:

  • Use the service to commit or facilitate crime (including fraud, unauthorised access, or dealing in stolen credentials).
  • Store other people’s personal information without a lawful right to do so.
  • Attack, scan, or overload the API, bypass rate limits, or attempt to decrypt other users’ blobs.
  • Upload malware, exploits, or content whose mere hosting would be unlawful in Victoria, Australia.
  • Misrepresent the product as a bank, certified custody service, or licensed financial advice.
  • Create accounts for people under 16, or share a master password in a way that makes you an unauthorised operator of someone else’s vault.

Zero-knowledge hosting does not make unlawful content lawful. If we receive a valid legal notice we may suspend an account, delete ciphertext we cannot read, and preserve records the law requires. We cannot inspect vault plaintext to “moderate” it; enforcement is on metadata, abuse of the API, and lawful process.

The Python CLI on your own machine is yours to run; these hosted-service rules apply to the website and API.
