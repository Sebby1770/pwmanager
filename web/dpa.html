Data processing terms · 12 September 2026

Processor terms for ciphertext only

These terms apply when you use the hosted Pro sync. They sit with the Terms and Privacy policy.

Roles

You are the controller of the vault contents you choose to encrypt on your device (passwords, notes, TOTP secrets, names). Sebby1770 / pwmanager is a processor only of the opaque ciphertext blob and a controller of limited account metadata (email, plan, hashed auth verifier, hashed IPs on audit events).

Because we cannot decrypt the blob, we cannot fulfil a plaintext access request against vault contents. We can delete the blob, export the envelope, and tell you what account metadata we hold.

Instructions

We process the blob only to store, version (Pro), retrieve, export, or delete it on your instruction via the API. We will not attempt to decrypt it. We will not sell it.

Sub-processors

  • Stripe — payment processing for Pro. Card data is Stripe’s PCI scope, not ours.

If you self-host, you choose the hosting sub-processors. A public demo, if offered, is best-effort and may run on whatever host the operator currently uses; treat it as a test instance, not a bank-grade custody platform.

Security measures

Described on the security page: TLS assumed in production, hashed verifiers, hashed session tokens, size limits, webhook HMAC, no plaintext password columns. These are proportionate to an independent project, not a certified ISMS.

Assistance

We will assist with deletion and ciphertext portability. We cannot search inside vaults. If a data-subject request concerns content inside the envelope, you must handle it — you hold the key.

Breach

If we become aware of a breach of personal information we hold (for example account emails or ciphertext at rest), we will notify you without undue delay and assess Australian NDB / GDPR duties as applicable. We still cannot decrypt your vault to see what was inside the blob.

Deletion and return

On account deletion we wipe blobs, revisions, and sessions, and tombstone the email. You should export an encrypted backup first. Payment event identifiers may remain for legal record-keeping without card objects.

Law

Victoria, Australia, without limiting mandatory privacy or consumer laws that apply to you.
